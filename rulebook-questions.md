# Point Grey Flag Football — rulebook gaps to raise at training

Condensed from the 2025 Rules & Regulations (rev. Jan 5, 2026) while building the
in-app quick reference. Section numbers refer to the rulebook. "NFL default" is the
tiebreaker Tom uses when the book is silent.

## Worth settling on Tuesday

1. **Illegal run / hand-off inside the 5 (7o).** The book says "rush penalty of 5 yards"
   and nothing about the down. But 7l.vi says *any* ball-carrier infraction is 5 yards
   **and loss of down**, and a QB scramble inside the 5 is a ball-carrier act.
   Proposed call: 5 yards + loss of down. Confirm.

2. **Muffed kickoff/punt (7f, 8e).** 8e says the receiving team may "pick up" a fumbled
   kick; it doesn't literally say they may *advance* it. Our practice is that they can
   return it unless there's collision risk, in which case it's blown dead at the spot.
   Confirm that a muff that is picked up cleanly can be returned for a TD.

3. ~~Time-outs when the clock is running (4b, 10).~~ **Resolved:** a time-out always stops
   the clock, running time or not (house call — it's the only reason anyone calls one).

4. **10-second run-off / last-10-seconds rule (4e.ii).** Applies to "any offensive
   penalty" in stop time. Can the defense decline the penalty and keep the run-off, or
   decline both (NFL: the defense may decline the run-off)? Also: does the run-off apply
   on a dead-ball foul like delay of game? The book doesn't distinguish.

5. **Defensive PI in the end zone (13e.ii).** "Automatic first down at the point of
   infraction" — if the foul is in the end zone, where is the ball placed? NFL default:
   the 1-yard line, first down.

6. **Punt halo violation (8b).** A 5-yard halo must be given to the receiver, but no
   penalty is listed for breaking it. Obstruction/interference (10 yards) or the
   5-yard "illegal rushing"? Proposed: 10-yard obstruction from the spot of the catch.

7. **Illegal forward pass spot (7i.viii vs 13d.v).** 7i says loss of down "from the point
   of the illegal pass"; 13d says 5 yards + loss of down. Proposed (and how the app
   reads it): 5 yards from the spot of the pass + loss of down, matching NFL.

8. **Intentional grounding (7i.iii, 13d.vi).** Only 5 yards + loss of down here (NFL: spot
   of the foul or 10 yards, plus loss of down). Confirm 5 yards from the previous spot.

9. ~~Down by contact (7l.v).~~ **Resolved, as written:** any part of the body other than
   the feet — a hand on the turf downs the carrier. Say it out loud; players assume NFL.

10. ~~One-foot catch (9c.ii).~~ **Resolved, as written:** control with one foot in bounds
    is a catch. No two-feet / survive-the-ground test — deliberately not the NFL rule.

11. **Convert after a TD on the final play (4e.i)** — played even at 0:00. Confirm this also
    applies at the end of the *first* half (the text says "period", so yes).

12. ~~Kickoff out of bounds (6e/6f).~~ **Resolved:** receivers' ball at their 15, no
    re-kick, no yardage. Very common — it's in the quick reference.

13. **Stripping the ball (9c.b).** Dead ball + 5 yards on the defense. Is the down
    replayed, or does the offense get the yardage tacked on to the end of the run?
    Proposed: tack on from the spot of the strip, replay the down.

14. **Intercepting a lateral (7e, 7k) — clarified as legal.** A lateral caught in the air by
    the defense never touches the ground, so it isn't a dead-ball fumble: it's a live
    turnover and can be returned. (Happened last season; we let it stand — no safety risk.)
    Worth stating explicitly at training so an unsure ref doesn't wave it off.

## Minor / editorial

- 12e cross-references "H12b or H12c" — should be C12b/C12c.
- 4c "Any other cause at the official's discretion" — several lines with apostrophes are
  in a different font in the PDF and drop out of text extraction; nothing wrong in print.
- Substitution rule 12c (must join the pre-snap huddle) — we don't police it.
- MVP selection (11b) — not done in practice.

## Things the book is clear on that people get wrong

- Hand-off behind the line **kills the steamboat count** immediately (7h).
- Open divisions rush from a **10-yard rush line**, no steamboats (9a.i).
- Interception on a convert returned all the way = **2 points** (7p.i.d).
- After a safety the team scored on **kicks off from its own 15** (7q.ii).
- A flag that falls off by accident → **one-hand touch** between shoulders and knees (9b.iv).
