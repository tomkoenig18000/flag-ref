# Flag Ref

Scoreboard, game clock, stop-time alerts and a quick-reference rulebook for refereeing UBC Point Grey Cup flag football. Runs as a home-screen app on any phone and works offline.

## Install

Open **https://tomkoenig18000.github.io/flag-ref/** on your phone, then add it to your home screen. It's free, needs no account or app store, and once it's been opened with signal it works offline on the field.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open the link in Safari (not inside another app's browser).
2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open it from the home screen icon, not from Safari.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the link in Chrome.
2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open it from the home screen icon.

**Tips**
- Launch it once with signal before the first game so it can save itself for offline use.
- Updates arrive on their own: open the app with signal and return to the home screen. The version number at the top right of the home screen (e.g. v30) shows which one you have.
- Games are saved on your phone. Tap the version number, then "Copy backup of everything", to keep a copy.

Source in `src/app.html`; `python3 build.py` produces the site in `docs/`; tests in `qa/`. See `CLAUDE.md` for the working notes.
